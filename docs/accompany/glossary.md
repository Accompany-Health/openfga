# Glossary (Accompany Health notes)

Standard OpenFGA/ReBAC terminology (store, tuple, relation, type, model,
check/expand/list-objects) is defined by upstream — see
[openfga.dev/docs/concepts](https://openfga.dev/docs/concepts) and
[`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md). No Accompany-specific terminology exists for
this repo beyond its role as an authorization-decision service consumed by
other Accompany services (see [`docs/accompany/dependencies.md`](dependencies.md)).

Org-wide terminology is maintained in `ah-agents`' central glossary.
